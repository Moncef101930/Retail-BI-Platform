# Business Understanding

## Overview

This document represents the first phase of the CRISP-DM methodology for the Retail BI Platform project.

The objective of the Business Understanding phase is to define the business context, identify the main business problems, establish the project objectives, and determine the key performance indicators (KPIs) required for decision-making.

The project is based on the Olist e-commerce dataset and aims to build a Business Intelligence platform capable of transforming raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights.

---

## Business Context

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of data related to customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, and deliveries.

Without a centralized analytical solution, this information can be difficult to analyze and use effectively for business decision-making.

The Retail BI Platform aims to address this problem by implementing a complete data pipeline that transforms raw operational data into a structured analytical environment.

---

## Business Problems

The project focuses on answering several important business questions:

- How are sales performing over time?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which sellers contribute the most to sales?
- What is the geographic distribution of customers?
- What are the most frequently used payment methods?
- How does freight cost affect the total order value?
- What is the evolution of order volume over time?
- Which factors influence customer satisfaction?
- How can business performance be monitored through interactive dashboards?

---

## Business Objectives

The main objectives of the project are:

1. Centralize and structure e-commerce data.
2. Build a reliable Data Warehouse using a Star Schema.
3. Integrate data from multiple Olist datasets.
4. Create meaningful business measures and KPIs.
5. Provide interactive dashboards for business analysis.
6. Facilitate data-driven decision-making.
7. Prepare a scalable architecture for future analytics and automation.

---

## Key Performance Indicators

The main KPIs identified for the project include:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Products
- Total Sellers
- Average Order Value
- Total Freight Value
- Total Payment Value
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Seller
- Sales by Customer Location
- Orders by Status
- Payment Method Distribution
- Customer Review Score

---

## Expected Benefits

The Retail BI Platform is expected to provide:

- Better visibility into business performance.
- Faster access to reliable information.
- Centralized and structured analytical data.
- Improved monitoring of sales and orders.
- Better understanding of customer and seller behavior.
- Interactive and intuitive business dashboards.
- A foundation for advanced analytics and automation.

---

## Methodology

The project follows the **CRISP-DM (Cross-Industry Standard Process for Data Mining)** methodology.

The main phases are:

1. Business Understanding
2. Data Understanding
3. Data Preparation
4. Modeling
5. Evaluation
6. Deployment

This document covers the **Business Understanding** phase.
